Above was one of my outputs and it seems inserting random numbers in rows was almost
2 times faster that inserting them through columns. I think the major reason is that
there are alot of Translation Lookaside Buffer misses with the column approach. Since you are
jumping almost 4000 elements each time you iterate vs staying on the page (for the page length)
in the row approach. 

The summing was similar because the pages are already in memory, so when summing each one, 
it ends up being similar (same) times.
